The N-band filterbank, as realised

Exact DTFT of the FIR kernels the metric actually applies — not the idealised shapes. Six one-dimensional channels per axis: a low-pass cornered at 0.8 cpd, band-passes peaking at 1.7, 2.8, 4 and 8 cpd, and a high-pass. The Barten contrast sensitivity function (30° field, 200 cd/m²) is drawn normalised to its own peak as a reference, on the same axis.
